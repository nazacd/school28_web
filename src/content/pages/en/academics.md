Our curriculum follows the national education standards and is enriched with languages, STEM and the arts.

## Primary school (grades 1–4)

Reading, writing, mathematics and the world around us, taught through play, projects and lots of encouragement.

## Middle school (grades 5–9)

A broad range of subjects including sciences, history, languages, information technology, art, music and physical education.

## Senior school (grades 10–11)

Deeper study and preparation for university entrance exams, with elective courses and career guidance.

## Languages

All students learn **Uzbek, Russian and English**. Advanced groups are available for motivated learners.

## Beyond the classroom

- Olympiad preparation in mathematics, physics and languages
- Robotics and programming clubs
- Art, music and theatre studios
- Football, basketball, volleyball and chess
