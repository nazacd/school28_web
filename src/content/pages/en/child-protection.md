The safety and wellbeing of every child is our highest priority. All staff and volunteers share responsibility for protecting children from harm.

## Our principles

- Every child has the right to be safe and to be heard.
- All concerns are taken seriously and handled confidentially.
- Staff receive regular child protection training.
- All staff undergo background checks before they start work.

## If you have a concern

If you are worried about a child's safety, please speak to the safeguarding lead or any member of staff as soon as possible. In an emergency, call the relevant emergency services immediately.

## Online safety

We teach students how to stay safe online and ask parents to talk with their children about responsible internet use.
