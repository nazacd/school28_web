Our school is a community of students, families, teachers and staff. These commitments describe how we treat each other and what everyone can expect from us.

## We commit to

1. **Treat every person with respect**, whatever their background, language, religion, ability or opinion.
2. **Speak and act honestly**, and take responsibility for our actions.
3. **Keep our school safe**, physically and emotionally, online and offline.
4. **Listen to each other** and solve disagreements calmly and fairly.
5. **Look after our environment**: our classrooms, our school grounds and our town.

## Students

Come to school ready to learn, help classmates and follow school rules.

## Parents and families

Stay in touch with teachers, support learning at home and take part in school life.

## Teachers and staff

Create engaging lessons, know each student as an individual and communicate openly with families.
