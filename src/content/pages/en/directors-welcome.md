Dear students, parents and guests,

Welcome to School No. 28! Thank you for your interest in our school.

> **Our School** is not only an educational institution where students gain knowledge, but also an important environment where the worldview of the younger generation is shaped, talents and abilities are discovered, and opportunities are created for them to pursue noble goals.
>
> **Our main goal** is to nurture a well-rounded generation of independent thinkers equipped with modern knowledge and skills, who feel responsible for the progress and prosperity of our Motherland.
>
> To achieve this goal, we work closely with dedicated teachers, active students, and parents who genuinely care about education. After all, quality education and proper upbringing are the results of effective cooperation between the school, family, and society.
>
> At our school, special attention is paid not only to students' academic achievements but also to developing their creative potential and encouraging their interests in sports, science, arts, and modern technologies.
>
> We believe that every child has unique talents. Our mission is to discover these talents, nurture them, and guide each student towards achieving great success.

For more than six decades our school has been a place where children discover their talents, make lifelong friends and prepare for the future. We are proud of our teachers, who combine deep knowledge of their subjects with real care for every student.

We believe that a good school is a partnership between students, families and teachers. We keep our doors open, listen to your ideas and work together to give every child the best possible start.

On this website you will find information about our programmes, admissions, events and everyday school life. If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us. We would be happy to welcome you in person.

With warm regards,

**Baysalova Ayjan Alibekovna**\
Head of School
