We welcome new students throughout the year, subject to available places.

## Admission steps

1. **Contact us** by phone, email or the [contact form](/en/contact/).
2. **Visit the school** and meet our team.
3. **Submit the documents** listed below.
4. **Receive confirmation** of your child's class and start date.

## Documents required

- Application form (available at the school office)
- Copy of the child's birth certificate
- Copy of a parent's passport
- Medical record (form 086)
- Photos 3×4 (2 pcs)
- Transfer documents (for students from other schools)

## Important dates

- **Grade 1 applications:** from 1 April
- **Open day:** see [events](/en/events/)
- **School year starts:** 2 September
