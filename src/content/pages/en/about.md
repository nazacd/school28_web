School No. 28 is a general secondary school serving families in our town for more than sixty years. Today we welcome over a thousand students from grade 1 to grade 11.

## Our mission

To give every child a strong education in a safe, caring environment, and to help them grow into confident, curious and responsible citizens.

## Our vision

A school where every student feels known, challenged and supported, and leaves ready for further study, work and life.

## Our values

- **Respect.** We treat everyone with kindness and dignity.
- **Curiosity.** We ask questions and love to learn.
- **Responsibility.** We take care of ourselves, each other and our school.
- **Excellence.** We do our best and keep improving.
- **Community.** We work together with families and our town.

## A short history

The school opened its doors in the 1960s with a handful of classrooms. Over the decades it has grown into a modern school with science laboratories, a library, a sports hall and computer rooms, while keeping the friendly atmosphere it has always been known for.
