## How to apply

Send your CV and a short cover letter to the school email address shown on the [contact page](/en/contact/), or use the contact form and choose the subject "Employment". Shortlisted candidates will be invited for an interview.

All appointments are subject to background checks in line with our [child protection policy](/en/child-protection/).
