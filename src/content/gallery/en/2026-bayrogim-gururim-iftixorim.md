Today, a festive event entitled **“My Flag — My Pride, My Honour!”** was held in a high-spirited atmosphere at School No. 28 in Qibray District to mark November 18 — the day the State Flag of the Republic of Uzbekistan was adopted.

During the event, students learned about the history and significance of our Motherland’s sacred symbol — the **State Flag**, as well as the noble values and ideas embodied in it.

Students presented patriotic songs, poems dedicated to our flag, impressive theatrical performances and artistic presentations. Each performance further strengthened the participants’ sense of national pride, love for the Motherland and honour.

The event was actively organized with the participation of school counsellor **Visola Karimovna**, IT teacher **Nigora Erkinovna**, Fine Arts teacher **Sanobar Kamilxodjayevna**, as well as the **leader and coordinators of the School Student Council**.

 **Our Flag is a symbol of our freedom, national pride and independence!**
