Dear students! The time has come to choose a responsible and proactive class representative who can speak on behalf of your class and unite their peers! 🌟

🗣 During the election, each candidate must introduce themselves and answer the following question:❓ *"Why should you elect me as the class representative?"*

In their speeches, candidates should explain:✅ Their strengths and abilities;✅ The initiatives they plan to implement for the class;✅ How they will protect the interests of the students;✅ What new ideas and changes they will bring to class life;✅ Why they are a deserving representative.

🗳 Remember! Being a class representative is not just a title—it is trust, responsibility, and leadership!

💡 Candidates — show what you are capable of!🤝 Voters — vote for the most deserving candidate!
