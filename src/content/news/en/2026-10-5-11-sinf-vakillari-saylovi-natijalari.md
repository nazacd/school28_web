Class representative elections were held among students in grades 5–11 at Secondary School No. 28 in Qibray district.

✨ According to the election results, active, enterprising, and knowledge-driven students chosen by their peers were elected as class representatives for each grade.

📌 A class representative is not just a title!

It means living up to classmates' trust, voicing their thoughts and ideas, actively participating in school life, and paving the way for new initiatives.

👏 Congratulations to all the elected class representatives! We wish them good luck, fresh ideas, and great success in their future endeavors!

💡 Remember: leadership is not about being above others, but about rising together with them!

💜 The future is not a coincidence. We build it starting today!
