🎨 3 age categories:



🔹 Grades 1–4 — “Young Little Artists”



🔹 Grades 5–7 — “Young Artists”



🔹 Grades 8–11 — “Young Designers”



Showcase your creativity through drawings, appliqué art, clay modelling, wall newspapers, 3D compositions, graphic design and video clips!

🏆 The best creative works will be displayed at the “MY MOTHERLAND’S FLAG” exhibition in the school lobby on November 18.

One winner from each category — 3 outstanding young creators in total — will receive a diploma and commemorative gifts from the School Counsellor!

💜 This is more than just a competition — it is a creative project that we aim to make a lasting school tradition!

We love our flag, cherish it and take pride in it!
